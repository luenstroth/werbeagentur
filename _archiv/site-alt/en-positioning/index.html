Positioning and approach of the advertising agency

What matters is the concept.

And securing the right placement in the market — with maximum credibility and persuasive power. Four pages on what that means in practice.

Most communication projects start with the means: a campaign, a relaunch, a website. The tool is fixed before the task is clear. Here the order is reversed — the starting point is the decision that is supposed to be made at the end, and everything else follows backwards from it.

This is not a matter of style but a response to a changed situation: execution — design, copy, images, code — has become fast and cheap. What has remained scarce is the well-founded decision about what to do and what to leave out, and the willingness to stand by it.

What matters is the concept — and securing the right placement in the market, with maximum credibility and persuasive power.

This sentence contains four statements. Each has its own page.

The four strengths at a glance

01

Thinking from the end

The method: start with the decision situation, not with the brief.

Who actually decides, where, within what time, against which alternative? From there a path leads backwards through retail, purchasing and specialist planning to what needs to be done. The means is settled at the end, not at the beginning — which is why what will be produced is open when a mandate starts.

Cobiax: the buying decision was made by the developer, not the structural engineer — which changed the claim.

02

Concept before execution

The service: a decision with a verifiable rationale, not a package of proposals.

Diagnosis and concept can be commissioned on their own, with a fixed scope — not as unbilled groundwork for a design commission. Part of this is “change follows strategy”: what the strategy demands is changed, nothing more. Recognition is an asset you do not write off without reason.

Gneuss: only the symbol went, the word mark stayed. Volksbank PHD: the acquired local brand was kept in full.

03

Placement in the market

The safeguard: a location in the competitive field, not a self-description.

Who are you compared against, on which shelf, in which tender, in which list of search results? Five factors decide: distance from competitors, sales channel, pricing latitude, robustness in trademark law and findability — including by generative systems that recommend brands without linking to them.

Albatros: too much like a private label for fair prices. Kühlmann: discount price, but no cheap look.

04

Credibility and persuasive power

The yardstick: plausibility can now be manufactured, credibility cannot.

It consists of verifiability, consistency over time and the courage to leave things out — decisions about content, not about wording. And persuasion is not the same as being liked. That does not mean dry: in many markets emotion is the product. What matters is whether it arises from the subject or is merely borrowed from the image.

Delikato: the fat content is printed on the pack variety by variety — even where the figure is less flattering.

How the four fit together

The order is not an outline but a sequence. Thinking from the end clarifies what the work is aiming at. Concept before execution decides what is done to get there and what is left out. Placement in the market determines where the brand should stand and whether that position is robust. Credibility and persuasive power is the yardstick against which every result is tested — and the reason some things that looked good are dropped.

The result is not a catalogue of services but a chain of decisions that can be traced backwards. That is the difference from a presence that is merely liked: you can disagree with it — with arguments, not with taste.
